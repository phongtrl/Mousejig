Using the pad

  1. Test your mouse. Lay the phone flat, tap “Test my mouse” and rest the mouse sensor over the textured area.
  2. Watch the PC pointer. It should move slightly during the eight-second test. If it stays still, turn up the phone brightness or try a different optical mouse.
  3. Start a session. Pick an interval shorter than your PC’s idle timeout, choose a duration, then tap “Start jiggling”. Leave the app open.

Some laser and optical sensors cannot track a phone display.
