Using the pad

  1. Test your mouse. Choose Fine grain, then tap “Test my mouse”. Lay the phone flat and rest the sensor over the moving pattern.
  2. Watch the PC pointer. The pattern shifts once a second for eight seconds. If the pointer stays still, increase the screen brightness and repeat with Wide bars. You can also try a single layer of thin paper over the screen if the pattern shows through.
  3. Start a session. Pick an interval shorter than your PC’s idle timeout, choose a duration, then tap “Start jiggling”. Leave the app open.

Some sensors cannot track a phone display.

With Vibrate, leave the mouse on its usual pad and rest the phone against its side. Use the test to see if each pulse moves the pointer.
